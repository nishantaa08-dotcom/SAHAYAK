THE MISSION

From Environmental Signals to Early Action

Landslide risk emerges from complex interactions between weather, terrain, soil, and human exposure. SAHAYAK turns these signals into decisions.

The Challenge

Landslide risk depends on multiple environmental and terrain conditions that evolve continuously.

  • ⬢Heavy rainfall events
  • ⬢Soil saturation thresholds
  • ⬢Steep and unstable terrain
  • ⬢Historical landslide patterns
  • ⬢Satellite-detected changes
  • ⬢Population and infrastructure exposure

The SAHAYAK Approach

A continuous intelligence pipeline from raw environmental signals to coordinated field response.

INTELLIGENCE LIFECYCLE

Predict → Explain → Prioritize → Warn → Verify → Respond → Learn

01

Predict

AI models analyze rainfall, soil, terrain, and satellite signals to estimate landslide risk.

02

Explain

Explainable AI reveals which factors are driving the risk at each location.

03

Prioritize

Rank locations by risk severity and exposure to focus limited resources.

04

Warn

Generate actionable warnings with clear severity and recommended actions.

05

Verify

Field officers confirm conditions and feed observations back into the system.

06

Respond

Coordinate evacuation, road closures, and relief deployment across agencies.

07

Learn

Outcomes refine the models — the system improves with every event.
